Networking

Enterprise Networking Solutions

Your network connects everything you work with: computers, billing machines, phones, cameras and cloud services. We design and install the switches, routers, wireless and security behind it, and plan it so it can grow from one office to several floors or branches.

  • Network switchesJoin your computers, printers, phones and cameras, and can power devices through the same cable.
  • RoutersConnect you to the internet and link your branches. A backup line can take over if the main one fails.
  • Wireless infrastructureAccess points placed by survey for strong Wi-Fi where you need it, with separate access for guests.
  • Network securityFirewalls and separate networks that help protect your data and control who can reach what.
Also part of this
  • Network design
  • Wi-Fi coverage planning
  • Secure branch and remote access (VPN)
  • Staff, guest and camera networks (VLAN)
  • Backup internet
  • Network monitoring

ELV systems

Phones, announcements, TV and building systems.

ELV stands for extra-low voltage. It covers the everyday systems in a building that run on low power and share the same routes through walls and ceilings. Planned together, they are tidier to install and easier to look after.
