On the ground

Hands-on engineering, from the patch panel to the platform.

The same team that terminates the cabling also provisions the cloud and builds the software that monitors it, giving you one point of accountability across the entire solution.

SwitchOps AI: network intelligence that stays on your premises.

Built on the DIMIC framework (Discovery, Inventory, Monitoring, Incident, Configuration), SwitchOps AI delivers complete network visibility and AI-assisted fault diagnosis, with AI processing kept entirely on your own infrastructure in the Local AI edition.

Designed for organisations that need modern network intelligence without sending operational data, credentials, or telemetry to a third-party cloud service.
