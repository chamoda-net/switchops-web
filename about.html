Who we are

One team, accountable end to end.

Founded in 2026, SwitchOps Tech is a Sri Lankan IT company serving clients island-wide across three practice areas: Network & Infrastructure, Cloud Solutions, and Software & Development. We build the physical networks organisations depend on, run the cloud platforms their services live on, and create the software that keeps it all visible and under control.

What sets us apart is continuity. The engineers who survey your site are the same people who configure your network, provision your cloud and support you afterwards. You get one point of accountability, and advice grounded in real deployments rather than vendor quotas.
