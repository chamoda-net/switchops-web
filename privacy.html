This Privacy Policy explains how SwitchOps Tech (Pvt) Ltd collects, uses, shares and protects personal data when you visit switchops.tech, contact us, request a quotation or trial, purchase a licence, or receive support. It is issued in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act, No. 9 of 2022, as amended by the Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act, No. 22 of 2025 ("PDPA"), and other applicable laws of Sri Lanka. We apply the standards in this Policy now, including to provisions of the PDPA whose operation date is yet to be appointed.

01Who we are (Controller)

1.1

The controller of your personal data is SwitchOps Tech (Pvt) Ltd, No. 36, Byrde Place, Colombo 06, Sri Lanka. Email: contact@switchops.tech.

1.2

Data protection enquiries are handled by our data protection team, reachable at contact@switchops.tech with the subject line "Data Protection".

02Scope: what this Policy does and does not cover

2.1

This Policy covers personal data that SwitchOps collects through the Website and our sales, licensing, billing and support activities.

2.2

The SwitchOps Software is installed and runs on the Customer's own infrastructure. Device configurations, credentials, logs, inventory, IP and MAC address tables and other Customer Data processed by the Software stay on the Customer's servers. SwitchOps does not receive or have access to that data unless the Customer deliberately sends it to us (for example, in a support ticket or a diagnostic file). For Customer Data inside the Software, the Customer is the controller and is responsible for its own PDPA compliance. When we handle Customer Data during support, we act as a processor on the Customer's instructions.

2.3

The licensing system works offline. The Software does not send usage, telemetry or analytics to SwitchOps.

05Third-Party AI Services

5.1

If a Customer enables a cloud AI provider (such as Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, OpenAI or DeepSeek) inside the Software, prompts and related content are sent directly from the Customer's installation to that provider, not through SwitchOps. That processing is governed by the provider's own terms and privacy policy and may take place outside Sri Lanka. The Customer, as controller, decides whether to enable it. The local-AI edition keeps AI processing within the Customer's network.

06Who we share personal data with

6.1

We share personal data only where necessary and only with:

  • Service providers acting as our processors (email, web hosting, accounting and cloud storage providers), under written contracts requiring confidentiality, security and processing only on our instructions;
  • Banks and payment institutions to receive and reconcile payments;
  • Professional advisers (auditors, Attorneys-at-Law and tax consultants), bound by confidentiality;
  • Authorised resellers or partners in your area, only where you have purchased through them or asked to be referred;
  • Government authorities, regulators, courts and law enforcement in Sri Lanka, including the Department of Inland Revenue and the Data Protection Authority, where required by law;
  • A successor entity in a merger, acquisition or restructuring, subject to this Policy.

07Cross-border transfers

7.1

Some of our service providers (for example, email or hosting) may store data outside Sri Lanka. We transfer personal data outside Sri Lanka only in accordance with the PDPA, namely to a jurisdiction recognised as adequate under the PDPA, or where we have put in place binding contractual safeguards that ensure compliance with the obligations of the PDPA, or where another condition permitted by the PDPA applies (such as your consent or performance of a contract with you).

7.2

Where a government or critical-infrastructure Customer requires data to remain within Sri Lanka, we will agree this in writing in the Order.

09Security

9.1

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised or unlawful processing, loss, destruction or damage, including encryption in transit, access controls on a need-to-know basis, multi-factor authentication for administrative systems, staff confidentiality obligations, and secure deletion.

9.2

No system is completely secure. You are responsible for keeping your own credentials and Licence Key secure.

10Personal data breach

10.1

If a personal data breach occurs that affects data we control, we will notify the Data Protection Authority of Sri Lanka in the manner and within the time required by the PDPA and the rules issued under it, and will inform affected individuals without undue delay where required.

10.2

If we are acting as processor for a Customer, we will notify that Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a breach affecting its data.

11Your rights

11.1

Subject to the PDPA and its permitted exceptions, you have the right to:

  1. Access: obtain confirmation of whether we process your personal data and a copy of it;
  2. Withdraw consent: at any time, where processing is based on consent, without affecting processing before withdrawal;
  3. Object: to processing based on our legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time;
  4. Rectification: have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected;
  5. Erasure: have data deleted where it is no longer needed, consent is withdrawn, or it was processed unlawfully, unless we must keep it by law;
  6. Review of automated decisions: although we do not make such decisions.
11.2

How to exercise your rights. Send a written request to contact@switchops.tech with the subject "Data Protection Request". We may ask for reasonable proof of identity. We will respond within the time required by the PDPA. If we refuse a request, we will give our reasons in writing and tell you how to appeal.

11.3

Complaints. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Authority of Sri Lanka (dpa.gov.lk).

12Cookies

12.1

The Website uses strictly necessary cookies for security and site operation, which do not require consent. Analytics or marketing cookies, if used, are set only after you opt in through the cookie banner, and you can change your choice at any time.

12.2

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some Website features may then not work.

13Marketing communications

13.1

We send product updates and marketing only with your consent. Every message includes an unsubscribe link, and you may opt out at any time by emailing contact@switchops.tech.

14Children

14.1

The Website and Software are intended for businesses and institutions. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18 years of age. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

15Links to other websites

15.1

The Website may link to third-party websites, including AI providers and vendors. We are not responsible for their privacy practices; please read their policies.

16Changes to this Policy

16.1

We may update this Policy to reflect changes in law, including the commencement of further provisions of the PDPA, or in our practices. The updated version will be published on this page with a new Effective Date. Where changes are material, we will notify Customers by email.
